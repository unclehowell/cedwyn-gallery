How this gallery is kept

The gallery has its own small database, gallery.json, built from the image inventory of the Wayback archive. Titles are as the inventory gives them. Each picture loads from the place that already holds it, so provenance stays with the original. Pictures listed in the inventory but not served by the site at the last check are counted above and not shown.

To trace a picture to its catalogue record, search the evidence catalogue with the picture's title or date.
